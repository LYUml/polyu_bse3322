\subhead{5.1\quad Minimum-height predictions and FDS verification}
A design must maintain 0.38~m at all eight stations throughout 120~s. We therefore evaluated each forecast against the lowest FDS reading in the scenario. Appendix~E reports time-series errors. In the first 20 cases on the 19-cell vertical grid, correcting the predicted minimum at each station reduced the mean absolute error (MAE) in the compartment minimum from 27.03 to 8.26~cm. The expanded comparison covered 27 completed FDS runs and 26 physical designs across OP-1 and OP-6. Each test withheld a whole design, including its mesh variants. MAE was 6.97~cm for the corrected PINN, 7.39~cm for direct regression and 6.70~cm for their equal-weight average. Of eight runs with a breach, the three models missed seven, four and four, respectively. Among 19 passing runs, they raised zero, one and zero false alarms. Table~F2 reports holdouts of entire inlet-layout families.

The open diamonds in Figure~5 show PINN forecasts frozen before the corresponding FDS runs. For OP-1 at soot yields 0.05 and 0.10, the PINN underestimated the minima by 5.1 and 34.8~mm, and the hybrid by 1.7 and 5.2~cm. At 1.12~m$^3$/s the controlling case was OP-6 at yield 0.05. The PINN predicted 0.5766~m and the hybrid 0.5576~m, respectively 140.8 and 121.8~mm above the FDS minimum. The six refined-grid FDS records establish the margin at this flow: the lowest sampled height was 0.4358~m, or 55.8~mm above the target (Table~D3).

\subhead{5.2\quad What the two search-order replays measured}
In the original layout, each query revealed the archived FDS result for one of 16 designs. The fixed order reached the design with the largest clearance margin on query 16, BO on query 7 and GA on queries 6--11 across 20 seeds.

In the revised layout, a separate replay examined which of six scenarios to check first at each flow. All methods tried flows in increasing power order. The fixed order checked OP-6 before OP-1 and used increasing soot yield within each door state. Its first check revealed the breach at 0.96~m$^3$/s. The next six verified 1.12~m$^3$/s. Ranking by predicted minimum height took 7--11 checks for the PINN, seven for direct regression and 7--9 for the hybrid. The ranges allow either outcome where an archived result was unavailable (Figure~C1).
